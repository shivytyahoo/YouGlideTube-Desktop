YOUGLIDETUBE DESKTOP CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.0.4
DATE: 2026-11-27
STATUS: RELEASE

ABOUT
YouGlideTube Desktop is the desktop version of the YouGlideTube Android app - the same fast, buttery-smooth YouTube client, with the same look, feel and features, rebuilt for the desktop. No ads, no analytics, no telemetry. Sign in with your Google account, or use it without one. This release adds the playback method selector and long read-ahead.

FEATURES IN THIS BUILD
- Playback method selector: pick how a video is played — Auto, DASH, SABR, HLS or Direct — from the player settings menu or Settings → Quality. A pick moves that method to the front and the rest stay as fallback, so a video still plays when the picked method has nothing for it; the choice applies to the next video from Settings and reloads the current video at its position from the player.
- Read further ahead: a new toggle in Settings → Network that lets the player buffer up to 120 seconds of the top quality, so 8K and other heavy videos keep playing through network dips instead of stalling.
- Video loudness matching is now on by default, so videos play at an even volume; turn it off in Settings → Player if you prefer the raw levels.
- Home key scrolls the page to the top on Home, Search, Watch and playlist pages; Home inside a text field still moves the caret.
- Equalizer gains stereo width, balance and night mode, for music and video alike.
- Per-channel auto-download: turn on "Save new videos" for a channel in Settings → Downloads and new uploads from that channel download automatically.
- "Save as one file" export on a finished download copies the video to a single self-contained file beside the downloads folder.
- Move download folder: relocate the whole downloads folder from Settings → Downloads, with rollback if anything fails.
